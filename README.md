## 🕹️ Project Overview
**ArcadeFlow 2D** is an open-source, distraction-free digital illustration and frame-by-frame 2D animation suite. It blends the tactile ergonomics of **Procreate** with the rapid muscle-memory of **gaming radial weapon wheels** and the vivid aesthetics of **Y2K retro-arcade neo-brutalism**.

---

## ⚡ Core Innovations & Features

### 1. 🎯 Gaming-Style Radial HUD (Prototype Inspired)
- **Zero Canvas Obstruction:** 100% full-bleed canvas with zero permanent toolbars.
- **On-Demand Polar Bloom:** Hold `Spacebar` or **Right-Click** anywhere on the canvas to open the 8-slot wheel.
- **Directional Flick Selection:** Equidistant tool sectors (*Pencil, Ink, Eraser, Shapes/Lasso, Timeline, Smudge, Paint, Charcoal*).

### 2. ⚡ Spring-Loaded Ergonomic Shortcuts
- **Hold `E`**: Momentary spring-loaded eraser. Releasing `E` snaps right back to your previous drawing tool.
- **Hold `Z`**: Momentary spring-loaded zoom. Scroll your mouse wheel or drag to zoom smoothly in and out. Releasing `Z` locks zoom and restores active tool.
- **`Q` or `X`**: Instant one-key swap between your current and previous tool.
- **`Ctrl + Z` / `Ctrl + Y`**: Multi-layer undo and redo.

### 3. 🖐️ Persistent Non-Dominant Hand Left Rail
- Always present across all modes (**Zen**, **Pro Studio**, **Animation**).
- High-tactility vertical sliders for **Size (2-80px)** and **Opacity (10-100%)**.
- Direct color picker disc and one-tap tool swap button.

### 4. 🖌️ Custom Brush Creator Lab
- Dedicated creator studio modal with **Tip Shapes** (*Round, Chisel, Ink Calligraphy, Charcoal Grain, Pixel Star, Splatter*) and **Grain Textures** (*Smooth, Paper Tooth, Canvas Weave, Halftone*).
- Live interactive **Scratchpad** to test strokes in real time before saving.

### 5. 🥞 Multi-Layer Compositing with Blend Modes
- Independent layers with visibility toggles and professional blend modes:
  - **Normal** (`source-over`)
  - **Multiply** (`multiply` - shading and comic flats)
  - **Screen** (`screen` - lighting and glow)
  - **Overlay** (`overlay` - contrast punch)
  - **Color Dodge** (`color-dodge` - neon luminescence)
  - **Color** (`color` - tinting lineart)

### 6. 📐 Geometric Shapes & Selection Tools
- Draw clean **Rectangles, Circles, Lines, and Stars**.
- Freehand **Lasso** selection tool with live preview.

### 7. 🎞️ Frame-by-Frame 2D Animation Dock
- Collapsible bottom timeline with play/pause looping, FPS control (6, 12, 24 FPS), frame duplication, and ghosted **onion-skinning**.
